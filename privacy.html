Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026. What we collect, why, and your rights.

1. Who we are

Sonaris is operated by Vinay, an individual seller. Contact us about privacy at support@sonaris.cc. This policy covers the Sonaris website, browser extension, and backend.

2. Data we collect

Account data. Email address, name (as entered at sign-up or from Google sign-in), account id, and email-verification status — needed to sign you in.

Usage data. Your plan, subtitle minutes used per day, and per-session records (a session id, billed seconds, and timestamps) — needed for quota enforcement and billing.

Payment linkage. Your payment processor's customer and subscription ids plus the current billing-period end date — needed to apply plans and run the self-serve cancellation portal. We never see or store card numbers: checkout and payment processing are handled entirely by our payment processor (see their privacy policy).

Support correspondence. Emails you send us — needed to help you.

On-device session. This site keeps your login session in your browser's local storage. We run no advertising trackers on the site.

3. What we do with your audio

Your audio is processed in real time to produce captions and is not kept afterwards: we store only the resulting usage counters and session metadata described above, not your audio or transcript text. Transcripts are delivered to your browser for display. An optional local debug recorder exists for troubleshooting and is off by default — it saves files only on your own machine, never to our servers.

4. Who processes your data

We use a small number of providers to run the Service, each processing only what it needs: our sign-in and database host for accounts, speech-transcription providers for live captioning, translation providers when translation features are enabled, and our payment processor for checkout and billing. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.

5. Retention and security

Account and usage data is kept while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards for billing records, fraud prevention, and legal compliance, then deleted or anonymized. Data travels over encrypted connections, and only our backend (never the extension or other users) can modify plans and quotas. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep access minimal and credentials server-side.

6. Your rights

Write to support@sonaris.cc to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to close your account (closing deletes your profile and linkage; past billing records may be retained as required by law). We respond to every request by hand. You also hold the rights granted by applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — for grievances, the same email reaches us directly.

7. Children

Sonaris is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact support@sonaris.cc and we will delete it.

8. International transfers

We are based in India and our providers host data in various regions, so your data may be processed outside your country. By using the Service you consent to that transfer as described here.

9. Changes

We will post material changes here and update the date above; significant changes will also be announced by email. See also our Terms & Conditions and Refund Policy.
