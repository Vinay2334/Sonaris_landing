Refund Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026. When you get money back, and how.

1. The short version

Sonaris offers a Free plan (40 minutes/day) so you can test the Service before subscribing. Paid subscription minutes are consumable on delivery, so subscription fees are non-refundable except where the law requires otherwise. Billing mistakes are the exception: if we (or Dodo Payments) charged you in error, you get that money back.

2. What is never refundable

Used or unused minutes from a current or past billing period, downgrades mid-period, and cancellations. Cancelling stops future renewals and you keep the paid plan until the end of the billing period — that remaining access is what the last charge paid for, so it is not refunded.

3. What is always refunded

Duplicate charges for the same period, charges after a cancellation that already took effect, charges for a plan you never held, and any other clear billing error on our side. Write to support@sonaris.cc and we will return the erroneous amount in full.

4. Failed renewals

If a renewal payment fails, you are not charged and your plan simply lapses to Free at the end of the paid period. There is nothing to refund — resubscribe whenever you like.

5. How to request a refund

Email support@sonaris.cc from your account email, telling us the charge date, amount, and what went wrong. We review every request by hand and reply within a few business days. Approved refunds are returned through our payment processor to your original payment method and typically arrive within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

6. Chargebacks

Please talk to us first: a chargeback freezes the dispute for weeks and may get your account suspended while it is pending. Genuine billing errors reported to support@sonaris.cc are refunded faster than any bank dispute.

7. Consumer rights

Nothing here limits rights you have under applicable law. If you are in the EU/UK, note that by purchasing you expressly consent to the Service starting immediately, and you acknowledge losing the 14-day withdrawal right once supply begins. Indian consumers retain their remedies under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

8. Contact

Billing questions of any kind: support@sonaris.cc. See also our Terms & Conditions.
